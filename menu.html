About the drinks

All espresso drinks use locally sourced Kalinga Apayao Robusta in medium and dark roasts. Food is coming soon; follow us for updates. Our signature lines are Tara Kape ("let's have coffee" in Filipino) for espresso drinks and Tara Matcha for layered matcha. Our Spanish Latte (espresso, condensed milk, milk) is a Philippine favorite, and the Strawberry Matcha is a customer favorite for photos.
